## Operation

`GET /me`

## Behavior

Returns what the API currently knows about your own account: profile fields including `base_currency` (the currency your calculations convert into; `null` means USD), the subscription active at the moment of the request (`null` when none), and your credit counters.

- `credit.total` is every credit granted to the account, `credit.used` what has been consumed, and `credit.remaining` the difference, never below `0`.
- These are the figures billing is applied against. They follow changes made in the dashboard within about a minute.

A `404` means your account has not reached the API yet; retry shortly.
