## Operation

`GET /websites/{code}/fee-schedule`

## Behavior

Returns the fees the platform currently publishes for one marketplace, one slot per purpose: `deposit`, `sale` and `withdraw`.

- Each published slot carries the fully expanded official calculation chain: every fee operator, convert operator and convert operator group with its values and currencies, so one request is enough to reproduce the platform's calculation.
- A slot whose chain has `id` `0` is explicitly free: the platform charges nothing for that purpose, and the chain has no steps.
- A slot is `null` when the platform has no official calculation chain published for that purpose: the fee is unknown, or the marketplace does not offer it. `null` never means free.
- Each chain states its `start_currency`: the currency a price must be in for the chain to apply (`null`: any currency).
- Ownership and management fields are not included.

A `404` means no marketplace has this code.
