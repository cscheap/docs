## Operation

`POST /fee-operators/{op_id}/fork`

## Behavior

Copies a fee operator into a new record of your own and returns it as revision 1, which you can then edit freely.

- You can fork any fee operator you can read: your own, official ones and those other users share. Otherwise 404.
- `revision` picks the revision to copy (the current one by default); `name` renames the copy.
- The copy is not shared, and its `forked_from` points at the source, whose `ref_count` increases by one.
