## Operation

`GET /fee-operators`

## Behavior

Returns the fee operators you can use in your calculation chains. A fee operator is one arithmetic step (`add`, `sub`, `mul` or `div` by a fixed `value`) applied to the running amount; `add` and `sub` carry the `currency_code` of their amount.

- Without `owner` the list holds every fee operator you can see: your own, official ones and those other users share. `owner` (`mine`, `official` or `public`) narrows it.
- `search` filters by a case-insensitive substring of the name; `sort` orders by `name` (default, official records first), `ref_count` or `updated_at`.
- Without `page` the whole list is returned; with `page` (and `page_size`, at most 100) one page with the `total`.

Deleted fee operators are never returned.
