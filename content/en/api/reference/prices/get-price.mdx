## Operation

`GET /prices`

## Behavior

Returns the current quotes for one item across marketplaces, identified by its Steam market hash name.

- The result has at most one quote per market and side, sorted by `market_code` and then `side`. When several sources report the same market, a quote that is still listed takes precedence over a delisted one, then data taken directly from that marketplace over aggregators, then the most recently confirmed quote (`fresh_at`) wins.
- A market is reported as delisted (`delisted_at` set, `quantity` `0`, last known price kept) only when every source for it has delisted the item.
- Items with enough market data also get one `CSCP` quote with `side` `mid`: the cs.cheap valuation, a weighted consensus of the marketplaces' sell listings and buy orders in CNY, refreshed once a day. Its `valuation` object carries the confidence (`credibility`, `uncertainty`) and the two consensus prices; on every other quote `valuation` is `null`. It is an estimate, not a tradable offer.
- Narrow the result with `markets` (comma-separated market codes; `CSCP` selects the valuation) and `side` (`ask`, `bid`, `mid` or `all`).
- `market_hash_name` must match exactly, including case.
- Prices are decimal strings in each marketplace's own currency (`market_currency_code`); nothing is converted.
- `404` means the item has no quotes at all for this `app_id`. An item whose quotes are all excluded by `markets` or `side` returns `200` with an empty `markets` list.

> [!note]
> This endpoint is billed per request. Failed requests, including `404`, are never charged; `meta.credit` reports the cost and your estimated remaining balance.
