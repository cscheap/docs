## Operation

`GET /fee-workspace`

## Behavior

Returns everything the fee workspace shows in one request: your home currency, whether you can save, the currencies you can use, and every marketplace with the calculation chain applied to each purpose.

- Each slot holds the platform's binding (`official`) and yours (`personal`), each expanded at its bound revision, and `applied`: `personal` while you have one and an active subscription, otherwise `official` if the platform has one, otherwise `none`.
- A binding that cannot be applied (`available: false`, `reason` `deprecated` or `missing`) is reported as it is; it is never silently replaced by the platform's.
- `official_sequence` and `personal_sequence` are the slots' `sequence` values to send back when you change them.
- `personal_writable` is `false` without an active subscription; reading stays available.
