## Operation

`POST /convert-operator-groups`

## Behavior

Creates a convert operator group and returns it as revision 1, with its members expanded.

- Each ID in `member_convert_operator_ids` must be a convert operator you may reference: your own, official or shared by another user. Every unavailable ID is listed in a 400 under `details.missing`; a repeated ID returns 400.
- At least one member, and every member must convert between the same two currencies: that pair is the group's, and it cannot be changed later. Otherwise 422, with the problems under `details.issues`.
- It is yours and not official; set `is_searchable` to share it.
