## Operation

`DELETE /convert-operator-groups/{op_id}`

## Behavior

Deletes one of your convert operator groups and returns it with `is_deleted: true`. After that it is gone for everyone. Its member convert operators are not deleted.

- 409: it is still in use — by the current revision of a chain that is not deprecated, or by a chain revision a marketplace slot is bound to.
- 409: it is visible but not yours. 404: it does not exist or is not visible to you.
