## Operation

`GET /convert-operator-groups`

## Behavior

Returns the convert operator groups you can use in your calculation chains. A group holds several convert operators for the same currency pair and picks one result by its `rule` (`min`, `max` or `avg`), for example to always take the best available rate, or the average of several exchanges' quotes.

- Without `owner` the list holds every group you can see: your own, official ones and those other users share. `owner` (`mine`, `official` or `public`) narrows it.
- `search` filters by a case-insensitive substring of the name; `sort` orders by `name` (default, official records first), `ref_count` or `updated_at`.
- Without `page` the whole list is returned; with `page` (and `page_size`, at most 100) one page with the `total`. Each group embeds its members in full.
