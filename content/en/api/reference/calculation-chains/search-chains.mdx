## Operation

`GET /calculation-chains/search`

## Behavior

Finds calculation chains to reuse by binding or forking them.

- Lists every chain you can see that is not deprecated: your own, official ones and those other users share. `owner` (`mine`, `official` or `public`) and `search` (a case-insensitive substring of the name) narrow it.
- `website` and `purpose` keep only chains some binding uses on that marketplace or for that purpose.
- Ordered by `ref_count`, most used first, unless `sort` says otherwise; with `page` (and `page_size`, at most 100) one page with the `total`.
- Each entry carries a readable `formula`: a nominal amount of 100 flowing through the chain, or, for a chain that fixes no currency, its steps applied to a symbolic input `x` (for example `x × 0.975`); fetch the full chain with `GET /calculation-chains/{calculation_chain_id}`.
