## Operation

`GET /calculation-chains/{calculation_chain_id}/events`

## Behavior

Returns what happened to a calculation chain you can read, oldest first: revisions published, the record it was copied from, its deprecation, and marketplace slots binding or unbinding it.

- The chain's owner sees every event; anyone else only `publish` events.
- A `404` means the chain is not visible to you.
