## Buy and track a product

Open Billing with a completed account. Choose a plan or credit pack, review the total and submit the order. Sufficient wallet funds complete the purchase directly; otherwise follow the hosted payment invoice. Match the invoice's currency, network and payment instructions.

Payment confirmation and product fulfillment are separate stages. An underpaid order can stay pending; use its continue-payment action or cancel it. Overpaid funds remain in the wallet. Canceling a pending order does not withdraw already credited funds; a late accepted payment also remains in the wallet.

## Other controls

Use a valid redemption code in the provided form. Review order status and purchased subscription dates instead of relying on the payment provider's page alone. The wallet currently supports site purchases, not transfers or withdrawals.

Do not open several replacement orders just because confirmation is delayed. See [billing troubleshooting](/docs/support/billing).
