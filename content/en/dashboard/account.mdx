## Sign in and complete your account

Email/password and Steam sign-in are supported; GitHub sign-in is available when configured. A Steam-only account initially has no verified real email. Open Settings to add and verify one before using gated dashboard features such as Billing and API keys.

Creating an email/password account does not automatically verify the address. Use the verification action in Settings and open the delivered link. Linking Steam does not verify an email address.

## Linked identities

Manage connected accounts in Settings. A provider identity already attached to another account cannot simply be linked again; separate accounts are not automatically merged. Keep at least one login method connected.

Settings also provides profile and password controls appropriate to your account. If you cannot access other sections, check completion and verification first. See [authentication troubleshooting](/docs/support/authentication).

## Overview and daily check-in

The dashboard overview shows daily check-in and spending-unlock progress. Check-in requires a completed account and the spending milestone shown on the page. Wallet top-ups alone do not count as product spending.

Once unlocked, check in once per UTC day. The current reward is 1–10 base credits, with an extra 5 on day 3 and 10 on day 7 of a seven-day cycle. Missing a day restarts the streak; completing day 7 starts another cycle. Other displayed unlock milestones do not mean their underlying tools are already available.
