## Prepaid products

The current catalogue contains trial, monthly and quarterly developer plans plus a credit pack. Plans provide a time window and included credits; the credit pack provides credits without a subscription window. Check the [pricing page](https://cs.cheap/en/pricing) and checkout for current prices, durations and included amounts.

Subscription windows are prepaid and do not renew automatically. Purchasing another plan in the same category queues its window after existing purchased time; it does not create simultaneous extra coverage. Credits included in the purchase are granted when fulfillment completes.

## Purchase flow

Wallet funds are used for a product order. If insufficient, the payment flow collects the required top-up through a hosted invoice. The amount requested can exceed the exact shortfall because a minimum top-up applies; excess stays in the wallet. Review the actual invoice before payment.

An order awaiting payment is not an active subscription. See [Billing in the dashboard](/docs/dashboard/billing).
