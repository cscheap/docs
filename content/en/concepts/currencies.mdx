## Keep the currency with the amount

Every quote keeps `market_price_amount` in its marketplace's currency, `market_currency_code`, and that amount is never converted. The converted amounts (`buy_amount`, `sale_amount`, `payout_amount`) and the `CSCP` valuation use one currency per response: the `currency` you request, else your account's base currency, else USD. Changing the base currency in the dashboard therefore changes the default currency of these converted amounts, not the marketplace prices. Use [currencies](/docs/api/reference/currencies/list-currencies) to inspect the official currency catalogue and its rate fields.

Money and rates are decimal strings. Use a decimal library for calculations and round only at a defined boundary. Do not parse money into binary floating-point and assume exact results.

## Different currency contexts

Fee processing and the CSCP valuation are computed in CNY internally; the API converts them into the response currency with the official reference rates. A converted amount can therefore differ from your own conversion made at another rate or time.

When converting a quote yourself, keep the original amount and currency, record the rate you used and label the result as your conversion. Exchange rates and marketplace fees can change independently.
