## Keep the currency with the amount

Ordinary price responses retain `market_currency_code`; changing a dashboard preference does not convert these API quotes. Use [currencies](/docs/api/reference/currencies/list-currencies) to inspect the official currency catalogue and its rate fields.

Money and rates are decimal strings. Use a decimal library for calculations and round only at a defined boundary. Do not parse money into binary floating-point and assume exact results.

## Different currency contexts

CSCP valuation is in CNY. Fee processing computes CNY purchase, sale and payout amounts internally, while platform configuration describes its own home currency. These are different contexts; the public price schema does not expose all internal fee amounts.

When converting a quote yourself, keep the original amount and currency, record the rate you used and label the result as your conversion. Exchange rates and marketplace fees can change independently.
