## Amount, side and quantity

An `ask` is a sell-listing quote; a `bid` is a buy-order quote. Ordinary quotes use the market's currency and do not include the separately computed CNY fee tiers. Amounts are decimal strings to preserve precision.

`quantity=null` means unknown; `quantity=0` means no available quantity in that row. `is_quantity_minimum=true` marks a lower bound. `is_all_items_same_price=false` means the quote does not describe the price of every unit; `null` means unknown.

## Missing and delisted prices

A source can report no amount, yielding `market_price_amount=null`. Delisting retains the last known amount but sets `quantity=0` and `delisted_at`. Do not turn either state into a zero-priced opportunity.

An item may also have a [CSCP estimate](/docs/concepts/valuation) with `side=mid` and no quantity. Apply your own freshness and availability checks before acting on data.
