## Poll deliberately

Read `recommended_interval_seconds` from `GET /endpoints`. It is polling guidance, not a freshness guarantee or a replacement for the request limit. Reuse results among your application's users rather than issuing identical requests for each view.

For exports, use `ETag` and `If-None-Match`. Polling faster than snapshot generation does not create newer data.

## Handle failures

On `429`, honor `Retry-After`. On transient transport errors or `503`, use bounded exponential backoff with jitter and a maximum attempt count. Do not automatically retry invalid parameters, invalid credentials or insufficient credits without fixing their cause.

Price routes read data, but successful requests can consume credits. If a response is lost, you may not know whether the server completed and charged it; retrying can incur another charge. Batch requests reduce overhead without removing this uncertainty. Log request IDs when available and avoid logging keys.
