## Check the request

Verify `app_id`, exact name, wear and other qualifiers. Remove `markets` and `side` filters temporarily. A single lookup returns `404` only if no price rows exist; filtered-empty and batch-unknown results can be successful empty arrays.

## Check the quote

Inspect `fresh_at`, `delisted_at`, quantity and currency together. An unchanged amount can still be recently confirmed. A retained delisted price is not an available offer. `null` quantity is unknown, not proof of no stock.

Marketplace coverage, upstream failures and name classification can all affect availability. Not every item has a CSCP valuation; `side=mid` can therefore be empty. Exports can lag direct queries because they are snapshots.

Check [service status](/docs/support/service-status). To report a discrepancy, include the app, canonical name, market, side, timestamps and a redacted response. A market catalogue entry alone is not evidence that every item must have a price.
