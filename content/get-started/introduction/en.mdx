description: "Get marketplace prices for your application, starting with one item."
---

cs.cheap lets your application read item prices from multiple marketplaces through one API. Start by querying a CS2 item, then choose batch requests or an export as your collection grows.

## Get your first price

Follow [your first request](/docs/get-started/first-request): sign in, copy your API key and run one command. You will learn how to recognize a successful response and fix common errors on the same page. You need a terminal with curl and credits or an active subscription.

Then [read the result](/docs/get-started/read-price-data) to identify the market, amount, currency and update time. You can leave the detailed concepts until you need them.

## Look up details when you need them

[API reference](/docs/api/overview) covers request parameters and response fields. The appendix explains [currencies](/docs/concepts/currencies), [freshness](/docs/concepts/freshness), [marketplace fees](/docs/concepts/marketplace-fees) and the [CSCP estimate](/docs/concepts/valuation).

Prices describe observed market data. Check their age and availability before using them to make a purchase decision.
