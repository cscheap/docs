---
title: "Read your price result"
description: "Find the price, currency and update time before comparing quotes."
---

After [your first request](/docs/get-started/first-request), read the entries in `data.markets`. Each entry describes one market and side.

## 1. Find the amount and currency

This is an illustrative excerpt of one quote, not a complete response or a current market price:

```json
{
  "market_code": "BUFF",
  "provider_code": "BUFF",
  "side": "ask",
  "market_price_amount": "100",
  "market_currency_code": "CNY",
  "quantity": 5,
  "fresh_at": "2026-11-06T00:00:00Z",
  "delisted_at": null
}
```

Read it as: BUFF reports a sell-listing quote of 100 CNY. `ask` means a sell listing; `bid` means a buy order. `market_code` is the marketplace, while `provider_code` tells you where the data came from.

Always keep `market_price_amount` with `market_currency_code`. An amount is a decimal string; `null` means unavailable, not zero.

## 2. Check whether the quote is usable

- Check `fresh_at` against the maximum age your application accepts. An old `changed_at` alone does not mean the data is stale.
- If `delisted_at` is set, the last price can still be present but the offer is no longer listed.
- `quantity=null` means unknown. `is_quantity_minimum=true` means at least that many units. Only `is_all_items_same_price=true` confirms all reported units share the price.

The example's quantity of 5 does not by itself mean you can buy five units for 100 CNY each.

## 3. Compare the right amounts

`market_price_amount` stays in the marketplace currency, even when you request `currency=USD`. The converted amounts use the response's `currency`:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `buy_amount` | Purchase cost after official deposit fees |
| `sale_amount` | Sale proceeds in the marketplace balance after official sale fees |
| `payout_amount` | Proceeds after official sale and withdrawal fees |

These amounts can be `null` when unavailable. Your personal fee settings do not change them. For a first integration, display the original price, its currency and `fresh_at`; add fee comparisons when needed.

A `CSCP` row with `side=mid` is a daily estimate, not a listing. The first-request example selects `ask`, so it leaves estimates out.
